## Sourcing Precision Castings in the Coimbatore Hub

The Coimbatore engineering cluster produces a significant portion of India's precision pump assemblies, automotive tier-2 components, and industrial motor housings. While the region is globally recognized for iron foundries, its **aluminum and zinc die-casting units** provide critical supply chains for domestic automotive OEMs and export markets.

When sourcing die-cast components, procurement teams must look past general capability statements and evaluate suppliers against specific production boundaries.

---

### 1. Process Selection: HPDC vs. LPDC vs. GDC

| Process | Typical Machine Range | Wall Thickness | Ideal Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Pressure Die Casting (HPDC)** | 120T to 1200T+ locking force | 1.5 mm – 4.0 mm | High-volume automotive brackets, transmission cases, motor bodies |
| **Low Pressure Die Casting (LPDC)** | Automated cycle machines | 3.0 mm – 8.0 mm | Structural components, alloy wheels, high-integrity pressure housings |
| **Gravity Die Casting (GDC)** | Manual / Tilt-pour stations | 3.5 mm – 15.0 mm | Pump impellers, manifold bodies, medium-run structural castings |

When evaluating an HPDC supplier, verify machine locking tonnages relative to your component's projected surface area and intensification pressures. A supplier with 250-ton cold chamber machines cannot deliver a defect-free automotive engine cover requiring 800 tons of clamping force.

---

### 2. Alloy Chemistry & Mechanical Properties

In the Coimbatore manufacturing corridor, the following aluminum alloys predominate:

* **ADC12 (Al-Si-Cu system):** The industry benchmark for automotive and general engineering. Delivers excellent castability, high dimensional stability, and good machinability.
* **LM6 (Al-Si12):** Specified where superior corrosion resistance and high ductility are non-negotiable, particularly in marine, chemical, and pump casing applications.
* **LM24:** Broadly comparable to ADC12, offering balanced tensile strength and pressure tightness.
* **AlSi10Mg:** Increasingly specified for high-stress automotive and electrical components requiring heat treatment (T6 condition).

Ensure your supplier operates an in-house **optical emission spectrometer (OES)** to verify melt chemistry before pouring, preventing iron or zinc contamination.

---

### 3. Critical Quality Verification Checklist

A compliant die-casting supplier should provide clear verification data for:

1. **Internal Porosity Control:** Squeeze-pin technology, vacuum-assisted casting, or certified X-ray radiography inspection for pressure-tight parts.
2. **In-House CNC/VMC Machining:** Tier-1 and Tier-2 buyers favor single-source suppliers capable of delivering fully finished components with 4-axis or 5-axis machining.
3. **Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM):** Verification of geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) reports against 3D CAD step files.
4. **Certifications:** **IATF 16949:2016** is mandatory for automotive component supply; **ISO 9001:2015** is standard for general industrial machinery.
