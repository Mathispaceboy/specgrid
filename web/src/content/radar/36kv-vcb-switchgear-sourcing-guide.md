## Sourcing 36kV Switchgear Beyond Brand Labels

In electrical distribution, procuring a 36kV Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) panel is rarely a standard off-the-shelf purchase. EPC contractors and plant electrical engineers frequently encounter a fundamental issue with standard supplier directories: suppliers display branded distributor badges, but technical manufacturing capabilities remain opaque.

When evaluating a manufacturer for primary distribution panels (11kV to 36kV class), technical procurement requires verifying three core engineering boundaries before commercial discussions begin:

---

### 1. Interrupter Integration & Insulation Architecture

When evaluating 36kV panels, confirm the exact breaker mechanism and enclosure architecture:

* **Air-Insulated Switchgear (AIS):** Standard for industrial substations with adequate floor footprint. Ensure phase-to-phase and phase-to-earth clearances strictly comply with IEC 62271-1. Where space constraints exist, verify if manufacturer-tested solid insulation or heat-shrinkable busbar sleeving is standard.
* **Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS):** Applied in corrosive, coastal, or space-constrained installations. Verify SF6 gas compartment leakage rates (< 0.1% per annum) and dual-pressure monitoring systems.
* **Vacuum Interrupter (VI) OEM Alignment:** Most regional panel builders integrate interrupters from established OEMs (such as CG Power, Schneider, or ABB) into custom-fabricated sheet steel enclosures. Verify whether the breaker cassette is fixed or draw-out, and inspect the mechanical interlock design.

---

### 2. Type-Test Certification Verification (CPRI / ERDA)

A panel builder cannot claim 36kV rating based solely on computational models. The complete panel assembly—not merely the individual vacuum interrupter—must hold type-test certificates from accredited laboratories (CPRI or ERDA) conforming to **IEC 62271-100 / IEC 62271-200** or **IS 13118**:

1. **Short-Time Withstand Current Test:** Typically 25kA or 31.5kA for 3 seconds.
2. **Internal Arc Classification (IAC):** Minimum IAC AFLR 25kA for 1 second to ensure operator safety during an internal fault.
3. **Lightning Impulse Withstand Voltage:** For 36kV systems, standard impulse withstand must be rated at 170 kV peak.
4. **Temperature Rise Limits:** Busbar joints must stay within allowable limits under rated continuous current (1250A to 2500A).

---

### 3. Fabrication & In-House Testing Capabilities

When sourcing from regional manufacturing clusters like Coimbatore, Salem, and Chennai, distinguish actual fabricators from assembly shops by verifying:

* **Sheet Metal Fabrication:** Minimum 2.0 mm CRCA or Aluzinc steel structure with a multi-tank pre-treatment powder coating process (minimum 80–100 microns thickness).
* **Enclosure Protection:** IP4X for indoor panels; IP54 / IP65 for outdoor kiosks or harsh industrial environments.
* **Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT):** Ensure the manufacturer maintains in-house high-voltage testing kits (power frequency withstand up to 70kV for 1 minute), primary injection sets, and contact resistance meters.

---

### Direct Technical Sourcing

Rather than routing requirements through intermediaries who broadcast requests to broad trader networks, evaluate manufacturers by directly matching their verified voltage ratings, testing credentials, and busbar configurations.
